Privacy Policy

Last updated: [10 aug 2026]

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Nexora Labs ("we", "us", "our") collects when you use our website, sign up for an account, or place an order, and how we use, store and protect it.

1. Information we collect

WhatWhen
Name, email addressWhen you create an account or check out
Phone numberWhen you check out
Project details (site type, features selected, notes)When you use the AI Builder and checkout
Payment confirmation details (not full card numbers)When you complete a payment via Razorpay
Basic usage data (pages visited, browser type)Automatically, while browsing the site

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process your order, calculate quotes, and collect payment.
  • To communicate with you about your order or respond to enquiries.
  • To improve our website and the accuracy of our AI Builder recommendations.

3. How we store your information

Account details and order information are stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Firestore Database), a third-party cloud service. Firebase stores data on secure servers and enforces access rules so that only you and Nexora Labs can access your own account and order data.

4. Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay, a third-party payment gateway. We do not store your full card, UPI, or banking details on our own servers — these are handled directly and securely by Razorpay in accordance with their own privacy and security practices.

5. Cookies and local storage

Our AI Builder uses your browser's local storage to temporarily remember your site type and feature selections as you move between pages, so your cart isn't lost. This data stays on your device and is cleared once your order is placed or you clear your browser data.

6. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share it with the third-party services necessary to operate our site — currently Firebase (for accounts/database) and Razorpay (for payments) — or where required by law.

7. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at Nexoralabs24@gmail.com. Note that we may need to retain order records for a period as required for accounting, tax, or legal purposes even after a deletion request.

8. Data security

We rely on the security measures provided by Firebase and Razorpay, both of which use industry-standard encryption for data in transit. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

9. Children's privacy

Our services are intended for use by individuals who are at least 18 years old or otherwise able to enter into a binding contract under Indian law. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

11. Contact us

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at Nexoralabs24@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
