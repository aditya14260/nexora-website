Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: [add the date you publish this]

This policy explains how cancellations and refunds work for orders placed with Nexora Labs, including our 50% advance payment model. It should be read alongside our Terms & Conditions.

1. How our payment model works

When you place an order through our AI Builder, you pay 50% of the total quoted price as an advance to confirm your order and reserve a build slot. The remaining 50% balance is due when your website is delivered, before final handover of source files, hosting credentials, or domain transfer.

2. Cancelling before work begins

If you cancel your order before we have started any design or development work on your project, you are eligible for a full refund of your advance payment, minus any payment gateway transaction fees already incurred.

3. Cancelling after work has begun

Once we have started design or development work, your advance payment covers the time and resources already committed to your project and becomes non-refundable. This reflects standard practice for custom development work, where effort is invested from the very first working day.

StageRefund eligibility
Before any work has startedFull refund of advance, minus gateway fees
After design/development has startedAdvance is non-refundable
After final delivery & full paymentNo refund — see Section 6 for support/fixes instead

4. If we are unable to deliver

In the rare case that we are unable to complete your project — for example, due to circumstances on our end — you will receive a full refund of any amount paid that exceeds the value of work already delivered to you, as reasonably assessed by us.

5. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout, via Razorpay, within 7–10 business days of approval. Processing times beyond that point are outside our control and depend on your bank or payment provider.

6. After delivery: fixes vs. new requests

Once your website is delivered and the final balance is paid, any agreed maintenance period (if included in your order) covers fixing genuine defects in what was delivered. New feature requests, redesigns, or scope changes after delivery are treated as new work and quoted separately — they are not covered by this refund policy.

7. How to request a cancellation or refund

Email us at nexoralabs24@gmail.com with your order details and the reason for your request. We aim to respond within 2 business days.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The version in effect at the time you place your order is the one that applies to that order.

9. Contact us

Questions about a specific order or this policy? Reach us at nexoralabs24@gmail.com or visit our Contact page.
