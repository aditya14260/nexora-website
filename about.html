ABOUT US

A small studio, run like a lab.

Nexora Labs designs and builds websites for businesses, stores, restaurants, agencies and individuals — with a process that treats every project like a considered experiment, not a template fill-in.

What we do

We design and build custom websites end-to-end — from the first sketch of an idea to a fully deployed, working site with authentication, databases and payments where needed. Clients configure their project through our AI Builder, choosing a site type and the features they need, and get a transparent, itemised quote instantly.

How we work

Every order runs through the same simple process: configure your site in the builder, review an itemised quote, pay a 20% advance to confirm your build slot, and pay the remaining balance when your site is delivered. No hidden line items get added after the fact.

What we build

  • Business & portfolio websites
  • E-commerce stores
  • Restaurant & hospitality sites
  • Real estate listing sites
  • Online course / education platforms
  • SaaS & startup landing pages
  • Personal blogs and event/wedding sites
